# Big On

A music player for Home Assistant. A clean front end and a simple install, on top of Music Assistant.

Big On does not replace your music server. It drives the one you already have. It talks to Music Assistant for your library, your search, your queue, and every player on your network. What it adds is the part Music Assistant never finished: a real UI and an install a normal person can complete.

## What you get

- A polished now playing, browse, search, and queue interface.
- Every player and every source you already configured, surfaced in one place.
- Heavy Comforter pre loaded. Install Big On and the Heavy Comforter catalog is already there.

## Install

1. Install Music Assistant if you have not already. Big On needs its backend.
2. Install Big On through HACS.
3. Add the card to a dashboard and pick your players in the card editor.

No YAML. No manual config files.

## Heavy Comforter

Big On ships pointed at the Heavy Comforter catalog hosted on heavycomforter.com. The music is the work of W. Joseph Johnson, performing as Heavy Comforter.
